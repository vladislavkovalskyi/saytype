# saytype built-in dictionary.
#
#   Written: heard, heard, ...
#
# "Written" is the spelling that gets inserted. Heard forms are how Whisper tends to write
# the term in Russian speech; the generator adds case endings ("в реакте", "с докером") and
# drops any form macOS knows as an ordinary Russian word. "!" keeps a heard form that is a
# Russian word (without case endings); "!!" keeps a loanword with its case endings (докер). A term with no heard forms still fixes spelling of
# Latin variants ("next js" → "Next.js") when it isn't a plain English word.
#
